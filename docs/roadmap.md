After the bounded energy release, full reference parity needs separately scoped larger mixed-use models, shared/VRF and other systems, DHW/lift/exceptional accounting, and only then applicable regional baseline/evidence rules. See the implementation plan for prerequisites and measurable limits. No automatic BEAM scores, full BIM, CFD or optimization are part of v0.4.

## Historical release boundaries (preserved)

The statements below describe the earlier sequence and exclusions. They do not prevent the explicitly requested new energy implementation track above.

Climate-based daylight is now explicitly authorized for v0.3 under `v0.3-spec.md`. Historical exclusions below describe earlier release boundaries, not a prohibition on this new scope. Beyond v0.3, one-zone EnergyPlus requires a proper thermal/zoning/material/HVAC model and engine/license validation; bounded IFC ingress requires schema/units/placement/loss/rights checks; richer geometry requires robust topology and independent tests; regional assessments require current criterion/applicability/rights/evidence audits. No later feature follows automatically from v0.3 completion.

Complete and evidence the static overcast daylight workflow before considering annual daylight, weather providers, energy engines, IFC, assessment modules, optimization or remote execution. No future feature is authorized by completing the v0.1 checklist.

Any future regional assessment (including BEAM Plus) is a separately versioned evidence consumer requiring a fresh rights, applicability, amendment, standard and evidence audit. A simulation value is not a criterion decision or formal certification. No regional thresholds belong in the universal core.
# v0.2 boundary and later modeling prerequisites

The bounded v0.2 workspace adds native desktop interaction around the existing parametric daylight model. Full NURBS/Brep editing and booleans require a separately selected/reviewed geometry kernel, topology/units/tolerance contracts, robust geometry validation, persistent identities and independent geometric regression fixtures. 3DM/DWG interoperability requires format/library licensing review, explicit import/export loss reporting and roundtrip fixtures. Grasshopper-style graphs require a versioned execution/dataflow model and deterministic dependency tracking. BIM authoring requires semantic/schema ownership and a separate scope decision. None of these is implemented as part of v0.2; annual daylight, energy and assessment disciplines retain their separate scientific and licensing prerequisites below.
