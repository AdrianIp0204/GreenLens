## Method and source findings

Historical method 2 uses diffuse/ground transport plus full solar-patch transport minus direct-only solar-patch transport plus finite-disc direct transmission. Its complete matched-sky and annual convergence checks fail the fixed thresholds. Historical provisional method 3 replaced the reflected solar patch term with explicit-source full transport minus the identical centroid-direct term, then adds finite-disc direct transmission. `gendaymtx -s` retains its luminous ground, including direct-horizontal ground reflection. A separate `gendaymtx -n -D ... -M ...` invocation exports solar sources: combining source export with `-s` would zero their radiance. The native generator regression verifies exact nonzero sources against the historical separate `-d` invocation. No additional exterior ground is present. The [scientific method](v0.3-scientific-method.md) records the complete decomposition, horizon policy, aperture culling and resource limits. Method 3 was not accepted; later method 4 findings are recorded below.

The [pinned gendaymtx source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/gen/gendaymtx.c), [EPW reader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/gen/loadEPW.c), [Reinhart basis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/cal/cal/reinhart.cal), [source sampling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/rt/srcsamp.c) and [source dispatch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/rt/srcsupp.c) define the implementation conventions. The [official matrix-methods tutorial](https://www.radiance-online.org/learning/tutorials/matrix-based-methods) describes the direct correction principle. No tutorial models or diagrams are copied.

The EPW midpoint is `hour - 0.5` in fixed local standard time; there is no daylight-saving adjustment or dropped nighttime interval. Direct EPW input preserves dew-point information that would otherwise require an explicit WEA policy. The actual basis test confirms north-first bins increasing towards east. The pinned irradiance coefficient convention already includes the projected hemisphere factor; no extra factor of pi is applied during matrix multiplication.

The full and direct-only commands use identical divisions, primary repetition, receiver scale and deterministic-sampling flags, but different bounce depths. The pinned [ambient sampler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/rt/ambcomp.c) hashes the parent ray serial number as well as the primary sample index. Extra secondary paths change those serial numbers and other sampling state. Matching command flags therefore does not guarantee paired first-hemisphere directions across the two processes. The limited earlier two-sensor pilot did not expose this complete-grid/year failure.
