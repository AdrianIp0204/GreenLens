# v0.3 climate daylight method — investigation contract

This is the specification for an implementation under investigation, not a validation result. The final recipe, profiles, resource limits and numerical evidence must replace provisional decisions only after native execution. Legacy static scientific conventions and tolerances remain unchanged in `scientific-validation.md` and `model-contract.md`.

## Fixed method boundaries

Fixed rectangular geometry/shading and neutral glass/diffuse materials; exactly 8,760 hourly non-leap intervals, <=256 annual sensors. World axes remain east/north/up; room rotation clockwise from true north, with south window at zero. Use visible RGB Radiance output converted by `179*(0.265074126*R + 0.670114631*G + 0.064811243*B)` to lux. This is not a generic conversion from broadband solar W/m².

Canonical weather interpretation initially targets EPW hourly interval accumulations ending at hours 1–24, minute 60. Store interval beginning/end, representative midpoint, fixed local-standard UTC offset, source years and non-leap ordinal independently. A 1-hour accumulation in Wh/m² has the same numeric value as its average W/m², but the unit and derivation must be explicit. Typical-year source years are provenance, not weekday semantics. Require an explicit supported source timestamp policy; incompatible or undeclared irradiation shifts must not be silently corrected. Final accepted provider policy follows source review.

The pinned gendaymtx source accepts EPW or WEA with different available inputs. Investigation must choose one canonical route and record luminous-efficacy/dew-point treatment. Direct comparisons reconstruct the same generated patch radiance and explicit sun, not a nominally similar sky from a different generator. Do not use `-u` to drop nighttime columns. No missing data may be converted to night.

The inspected implementation selects normalized EPW as the canonical route, retaining its dew point. The supported PVGIS TMY profile declares an irradiation offset of -0.5 h relative to the interval end; its minute-0 label is normalized explicitly to minute 60 while original bytes remain intact. Generic hour-ending files require minute 60 and an explicitly selected midpoint policy. Other declared offsets are rejected. The pinned reader uses midpoint hour and a trailing three-record dew-point mean. A selected-hour generation must therefore retain up to two preceding records and select the requested column; generating only one raw EPW record would change the efficacy state.

The native adapter uses direct `gendaymtx`, `oconv`, `rcontrib`, `dctimestep` and `rtrace` calls with argument arrays. Inspected Windows `rfluxmtx` invokes shell commands internally, so it is not selected for production.

## Candidate method 5: paired external primary rays

The new source investigation is `perez-epw-dewpoint3-reinhart-hammersley-indirect-finite-direct/5`, adapter `radiance-climate-paired-primary-finite-direct/5`, recipe `greenlens.annual-recipe.v5`. **It has not passed release acceptance.** Its purpose is to remove the independent-primary mismatch exposed by method 4. The exact integration contract and retained native bridge evidence are in [the paired-primary integration record](v0.3-paired-adapter-integration.md).

For each authoritative upward sensor, an unscrambled cosine-weighted Hammersley sequence covers the entire upper hemisphere. At index `i=0..N-1`, `r=sqrt((i+0.5)/N)`, `phi=2*pi*radical_inverse_base2(i)` and direction is `(r*cos(phi),r*sin(phi),sqrt(1-r*r))`. Origins are shifted 0.0001 m upward. No aperture culling is applied to these reflected-light primaries. One uninterrupted sensor-major stream feeds each full/direct `rcontrib` process; the two streams must have identical byte, record and SHA-256 receipts. The direct coefficient pass uses `ab=0`; neither pass uses `-I+`.

With receiver radiance `k=100` and `-c N` already averaging each sensor's primary records, assembly is `pi/k * (C D + C S - C0 S) + F`. There is no second division by N. Reject every negative/nonfinite full-minus-direct coefficient and assembled reflected term. Diffuse/ground treatment, emitted-source time gating, finite-disc direct visibility and the RGB-to-lux convention remain as documented below. Paired directions do not make stochastic secondary paths byte-identical or prove convergence. Reflected solar transport still uses patches; direct sunlight retains explicit finite-disc geometry.

The predeclared candidate profiles are MF2, N16,384, full `ab7/ad64`, direct `ab0/ad64`, and 1,024 finite-sun directions; and an **annual reference** of MF4, N65,536, full `ab8/ad128`, direct `ab0/ad128`, and 4,096 finite-sun directions. Both use one worker, `lr=-16`, `lw=1e-9`, scale 100 and no reused ambient cache. The higher **MF2** research tuple is separate from that MF4 annual reference and cannot qualify its convergence or resources. The thresholds are unchanged.

The actual Rust stream/generator bridge passed 12 declared native cases at two sensor positions, including uniform normalization, black cancellation, A/B coupling and independent unglazed-aperture integration. The research A/B candidate also completed all 576 selected-hour values and independent arithmetic reads. These establish limited analytical behavior and integration, not qualified matched-sky agreement or annual performance. Complete controlled references, sensitivities, full-year profile convergence, installed delivery and final-source CI remain open. Historical methods 2–4 keep their exact recipes and failed evidence.

## Historical method 4: repeated coefficients with finite direct sunlight

The retained candidate is `perez-epw-dewpoint3-reinhart-repeated-patch-indirect-finite-direct/4`. It is **not accepted for release**. Native diagnostics found high-sampling float accumulation, a contribution cutoff at low local radiance, and sensitivity to cumulative ray-weight termination. These are distinct effects; raising one ambient-division count or scaling the receiver alone did not resolve them. Failed method-2/3 checks and the failed reference investigations remain in the evidence ledger.

Both complete-year shipping attempts subsequently failed before promotion: the independently sampled full/direct subtraction produced negative reflected-solar values at 148 A sensor-hours and 39 B sensor-hours. Their worst residuals were −187.9142 and −51.30498 lx. Successful required process exits did not make these valid results; the [retained negative-residual audit](evidence/v0.3-engine/method4-annual-negative-residuals.json) records the failures, and no value was clamped. The paired external-primary investigation uses identical explicit directions for the full/direct passes. Its aperture, coupling and stream checks remain limited research evidence; controlled matched-hour references and full-year convergence are still required before adopting a replacement.

Method 4 tests the direct-corrected decomposition `(C D + C S - C0 S) / k + F`, with `k=100`. A separate coefficient receiver has identical neutral RGB radiance `k`; `rcontrib -V+` therefore records scaled contributions. Contiguous repeated primary records are averaged by `-c N`, producing exactly one row per authoritative sensor. The Rust adapter divides each matrix product by `k` exactly once in double precision. Finite-direct tracing retains its original separate **unit** receiver and receives no scale factor. Both full and direct-only coefficient commands use the same repeated sensor order and scale; direct-only uses `ab=1`. Signed reflected-solar residuals must be finite and nonnegative; a failed correction is reported, never silently clamped.

The provisional shipping tuple is MF2, `ab=8`, `ad=8192`, 128 primary repetitions per sensor and 1,024 finite-disc directions. The reference tuple is MF4, `ab=9`, `ad=65536`, 32 primary repetitions and 4,096 finite-disc directions. Both use `lr=-16`, `lw=1e-9`, one worker, no reused ambient cache and the other explicit settings retained below. Negative depth enables the pinned engine's Russian-roulette low-weight policy while retaining a depth cap; it does not prove convergence. The two tuples do not have equivalent deeper sampling merely because their nominal sample products are similar.

This candidate still approximates **reflected** solar transport with patches. Actual direct sunlight uses the finite disc, glazing and exact aperture/shading/context visibility. A two-sensor pilot motivates testing the revised coefficient estimator but cannot establish its annual shading accuracy. All 576 prescribed matched-hour values, the independent reference qualification in [the sealed protocol](v0.3-reference-protocol.md), analytical fixtures, all four full-year runs, numerical convergence and installed delivery remain required before profile acceptance. The MF4 annual reference is a different-basis convergence study, not the MF2 identical-sky reference.

Methods 2 and 3 retain their complete serialized profiles, numerical branches, policy strings and recipe identities for historical inspection and replay. Their failed results are not relabeled method 4 or displayed as current method-4 evidence.

## Historical method 3: explicit reflected sunlight

The retained `perez-epw-dewpoint3-reinhart-explicit-indirect-finite-direct/3` investigation did not establish accepted accuracy or an annual resource budget. It combines diffuse/ground coefficients `C`, diffuse/ground patch radiance `D`, explicit-source transport including all configured reflections `Sfull`, matching centroid-direct explicit-source transport `S0`, and finite-disc direct irradiance `F`:

`C D + (Sfull - S0) + F`.

Each emitted solar source has its own retained time index and RGB radiance. Both explicit transport passes use the same source definitions and scene with no luminous dome or exterior ground polygon. The direct pass uses `-ab 0`; subtraction removes the centroid-direct contribution before finite-disc directional transmission replaces it. Indirect solar transport remains centroid-source sampled and subject to the matched-sky and annual convergence checks. It is not distributed over coarse sky patches.

The diffuse/ground matrix is generated with `gendaymtx -s`; a separate `gendaymtx -n -D ... -M ...` call generates solar sources. The separate call matters: the pinned implementation's `-s` also zeros exported solar radiance. A required native regression checks that the new solar-only generation preserves the exact nonzero source definitions produced by the previous `-d` route. Ground appears once in `D`, including its diffuse and direct-horizontal reflected components. The generator's emitted-source horizon policy still applies; a positive recorded DNI value alone does not prove that a source is emitted above the horizon.

Provisional source constructors use MF2/eight bounces/1,048,576 diffuse samples/8,192 explicit-solar samples/1,024 finite-disc samples for shipping, and MF4/nine bounces/2,097,152 diffuse samples/16,384 explicit-solar samples/4,096 finite-disc samples for reference. These are investigation settings, not a frozen accepted profile. Both retain one worker and the explicit zero-cache numerical settings listed below. Full-grid sampling, all predetermined matched hours for both designs, full-year convergence and actual resource measurements must pass before profile acceptance. Annual source transport may take hours; small-source timing is not a full-year benchmark.

The exact method-2/3 transport branches, complete settings and serialized profile bytes are preserved for historical inspection and replay. Unknown versions or cross-version recipes are rejected; accepting an old recipe never labels its result current for method 5. See `v0.3-contracts.md`.

## Historical method 2: failed patch-reflected solar candidate

Method 2 implements a direct-solar correction of matrix transport, following the decomposition in Appendix B.2 of the official matrix-method guidance. With full transport coefficients `C`, direct-only coefficients `C0`, diffuse/ground patch radiance `D`, solar-only patch radiance `S`, and finite-disc direct irradiance `F`, it assembles RGB irradiance as:

`C D + (C S - C0 S) + F`.

`D` comes from `gendaymtx -s`, including its entire luminous ground term. `S` comes from `gendaymtx -d`, which has zero diffuse sky and zero ground. The model has no additional exterior ground polygon or luminous ground term. Thus ground appears once. Full coefficients retain model interreflection. Direct-only coefficients use `-I+ -ab 1` with zero ambient value; for this release's diffuse opaque materials and simplified glass, a native 24-sensor check found these coefficients exactly equal to those from blackened opaque geometry with unchanged glazing. The parenthesized term approximates reflected solar transport using patches; it is not exact continuous-sun interreflection.

The solar patch correction is applied only to intervals for which the pinned generator emits an explicit solar source. Its patch-writing and source-emission horizon tests differ: 46 positive-DNI intervals in the investigated weather file have no emitted source. For these intervals the result retains `C D` and does not add coarse patch solar transport or finite direct sun. Original weather is unchanged. The recipe preserves all emitted source identifiers, including zero-radiance sources, and maps selected-hour columns to original interval ordinals.

Finite direct irradiance integrates equal-solid-angle directions over each emitted 0.533-degree solar disc. Provisional shipping/reference quadratures use 1,024/4,096 directions, respectively. Each direction is weighted by its cap solid angle, the upward sensor cosine and all three source RGB channels. Native `rtrace` evaluates directional transmission through glazing, overhang and obstruction. Rays start at the sensor's effective `-I` point, 0.0001 m along its normal. Each ray is conservatively culled only if it misses the sole validated aperture, lies below the horizon, or has no positive sensor cosine/source radiance. Inclusive aperture edge allowance is 1e-9 m. This optimization relies on the closed, diffuse-opaque, one-window room restriction; it is not a general multi-aperture transport shortcut. Eleven native shadow-fixture points gave equal all-ray and aperture-culled values. Batch ordering, counts and input/output hashes are retained; batch text is disposable scratch. RGB subtraction is not silently clamped. Non-finite or negative completed RGB values are rejected before results are finalized.

Direct sunlight must retain shadow geometry; no all-direct coarse patch distribution. Indirect reflected sun remains included. Record basis, orientation, normalization, column mapping and horizon semantics from pinned source and measured fixtures. ASCII interchange is mandatory until a Windows binary roundtrip is independently observed.

An independent finite-disc shadow fixture exposed the initial centroid-sampling limit. Pinned `srcsupp.c` assigns distant sources to a no-subdivision function, so reducing `-ds` does not resolve their penumbra with `-dj 0`. Jittered direct-only sampling passed a centered half-disc test but showed a persistent off-center discrepancy in `srcsamp.c`'s approximate square-to-disc mapping. Both failed approaches remain investigation evidence, not accepted physics. A 256-direction equal-solid-angle profile also failed an offset edge; the 1,024/4,096-direction fixtures passed their declared limits.

The separately identified horizontal-overhang and detached-context edge fixtures use nine sensor offsets each and elevated source radiance. Both declared disc sample counts pass every fixed per-sensor tolerance. Their independent reference integrates the projected solar disc using exact ray/plane boundary equations and verifies quadrature convergence. These direct-visibility checks do not establish reflected-solar accuracy, which remains subject to matched-sky and full-year convergence requirements.

An earlier explicit-source probe retained centroid-sampled reflected solar transport. A 128-positive-source, 24-sensor native run took 350.160 seconds; proportional scaling estimated 3.08 hours for that probe's full-year solar transport alone. That is an estimate, not a measured method-3 full-year runtime. The retained method-2 failures motivate the explicit transport investigation above; no requirement imposes a maximum wall time.

The method-2 Rust adapter completed a 24-sensor, 8,760-interval resource probe in 317.948 seconds under concurrent verification load. It produced all 210,240 finite, nonnegative lux values, with observed peak child working set 68,231,168 bytes, sampled runner-plus-child working set 78,712,832 bytes and whole scratch peak 136,546,918 bytes. Of 108,404,736 attempted finite-disc directions, 3,733,872 were traced. This establishes practical native execution of that historical profile and supported UI integration; it is not method-3 performance, an accuracy result or a user-facing runtime promise. Resource caps remain 2 GiB scratch, 512 MiB per generated matrix/file and 2 GiB observed runner-plus-engine working set. The working-set sum counts shared pages twice and is not total private memory.

The historical method-2 shipping profile uses MF2, eight ambient bounces, 131,072 full/direct coefficient samples and 1,024 disc samples. Its reference uses MF4, nine bounces, 262,144 full/direct coefficient samples and 4,096 disc samples. Both use one worker, `-aa 0 -as 0 -av 0 0 0 -lw 1e-9 -lr 16 -dt 0 -dc 1 -dj 0 -ds 0.05 -st 0 -ss 0 -u-`; direct-only patch coefficients override bounce depth to one. Exact complete argument arrays remain in the recipe. These settings stay stable for historical replay, but failed scientific acceptance. December interval 8504 exceeds the unchanged matched-sky tolerance with both MF2 and MF4 reflected-solar prototypes. Increased basis resolution alone did not resolve it. The retained failures and reference sampling investigation must be resolved before numerical acceptance.

The first complete shared-service A/B shipping/reference comparison also fails the annual metric requirement: A sensor `s000023` has DA 38.79452055% at shipping settings and 41.06849315% at reference settings, a 2.27397260 percentage-point difference against the fixed 2-point limit. The other 239 metric checks and all 96 UDI partition checks passed. This remains a failed candidate comparison; passing subsets do not establish profile acceptance.

## Metrics and identity

Daily default occupancy [08:00,18:00) uses interval overlap weights in fixed local standard time and yields 3,650 occupied hours. Explicit 8,760 weights lie in [0,1]; partial occupancy is supported and occupied darkness remains in denominator. Reject zero total weight. DA includes E=T; cDA awards min(E/T,1); UDI includes both L and U, under excludes L and over excludes U. Defaults 300/100/3,000 lx are editable conventions, not standards. See the exact equations and 1e-9 percentage-point arithmetic tolerance in `v0.3-spec.md`.

Physical identity binds original/normalized weather, interval policy, scientific geometry/sensors, engine, sky route and transport settings. Derived metric identity additionally binds occupancy weights/thresholds and calculation version. Presentation state binds neither. Comparisons validate all relevant identities; metric differences are percentage points, sensor summaries are explicitly not area percentages.

Replay <=max(0.5 lx,0.5% reference); same-sky analytical exterior normalization <=1%; selected-hour matched direct tracing <=max(5 lx,5%) at every sensor; annual shipping/reference DA/cDA/every UDI band <=2 pp at every sensor; arithmetic/UDI partition <=1e-9 pp. Preserve legacy A–F limits.
