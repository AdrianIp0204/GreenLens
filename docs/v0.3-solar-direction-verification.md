# EPW solar-direction verification

This bounded native fixture checks weather timestamps, coordinate signs and generated solar directions. It does not establish illuminance accuracy or precise astronomical ephemerides, and does not replace the frozen v0.3 irradiance checks.

## Independent reference and declared limits

The comparator independently implements NOAA's fractional-year approximation for declination and equation of time, then converts hour angle to east/north/up vector components. It does not call or reproduce Radiance's `sdec`, `stadj`, `salt` or `sazi` functions. The [official NOAA equations](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF) use east-positive longitude and a fixed UTC offset in hours. One degree is the **predeclared** maximum vector-angle difference between these distinct approximate ephemerides. This detects hour, midpoint and sign mistakes; it is not a claim of sub-degree physical accuracy at every place or date.

Equivalent longitude/clock/UTC-offset pairs must additionally agree within 0.0002 degrees, matching the generated source's six-decimal direction precision. Solar modifier minute IDs must match exactly. Printed direction length must differ from unity by at most 0.000002. These limits and every case are written to `declaration.json` before the first engine process starts. Failures retain the declaration, raw output and process records.

The engine source converts EPW east-positive longitude and offset to its internal west-positive convention. Its EPW calculation uses non-leap ordinal dates and representative midpoint hours; it does not select the alternative year-specific astronomical routine in `sun.c`. The fixture invokes no horizon-time adjustment and applies no operating-system timezone or daylight-saving rule. These statements follow the pinned [gendaymtx source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/gen/gendaymtx.c) and [solar helper source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LBNL-ETA/Radiance/rad6R0P2/src/gen/sun.c).

The cases cover equator/March equinox morning through evening; east and west longitudes; positive, negative and half-hour UTC offsets; northern and southern summer; and hour 24 followed by hour 1 of the next day. Artificial longitude ±180 degrees with UTC+0 places the local-midnight records in sunlight, making their direction and IDs observable. This is an intentionally unusual parser fixture, not a recommended site/timezone pairing. Source-presence checks are more than three degrees from the horizon; partial solar-disc emergence and atmospheric refraction are not tested here.

The original synthetic EPWs contain one or two complete local days and constant arbitrary weather values at tested daylight records. They pass directly to the native engine and deliberately bypass the application's full-year importer. They are not valid 8,760-hour application weather assets and make no climatic claim. All library-path configuration is limited to child processes, and the pinned executable hashes are checked before execution.

## Reproduce

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File scripts/annual-solar-directions.ps1
```

The default output is a new timestamped directory under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/GreenLens/verification/v0.3 solar directions 空間/`. An explicitly supplied `-SolarOutputDirectory` must be empty; existing evidence is never overwritten. `result.json` retains every expected and actual vector, angular error, offset comparison and hour-24 mapping. `processes.json`, original synthetic EPWs, solar source files, modifier lists, stdout and checksums remain beside it. No engine binaries are exported.
