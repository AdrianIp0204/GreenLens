## Install and launch

First save and close an existing GreenLens session. The candidate shares the product's installation registration; a second destination is not an isolated installation. The existing installed application was preserved during verification because native automation failed before any input. The [native checklist](v0.4-native-verification.md) identifies the remaining observations.

```powershell
$delivery = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA 'GreenLens/releases/0.4.0/20261013-energy-candidate-d144831'
& (Join-Path $delivery 'GreenLens_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe')
# After installation:
& "$env:LOCALAPPDATA/Programs/GreenLens/greenlens-desktop.exe"
```

WebView2 and the Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime are prerequisites; see [installation](installation.md). From the checkout, acquire the separately pinned engines/renderer once:

```powershell
pwsh -File scripts/install-energyplus.ps1
pwsh -File scripts/install-typst.ps1
```

Inspecting existing results or opening HTML/PDF needs no simulation engine. Generating PDF/PNG needs the pinned Typst/fonts; replay needs EnergyPlus 26.2.0. Ordinary calculations and reporting use local data, with no mandatory network connection.

## Inspect and reproduce the delivered example

```powershell
$delivery = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA 'GreenLens/releases/0.4.0/20261013-energy-candidate-d144831'
$cli = Join-Path $delivery 'GreenLens-CLI-0.4.0-windows-x64/greenlens.exe'
$project = Join-Path $delivery 'example-project'
$a = 'd7032960-4a14-4158-a276-af515bdf534a'
$b = '910f87da-3ba4-4edc-808a-0af2d5354a3e'
& $cli energy inspect $project $a
& $cli energy compare $project $a $b
& $cli energy rows $project $b --start 0 --count 144
& $cli energy package-inspect (Join-Path $delivery 'energy-example-evidence.zip')
& $cli energy replay (Join-Path $delivery 'energy-example-evidence.zip')
# Report and export destinations must be new:
& $cli energy report $project $a $b (Join-Path $delivery 'regenerated-reports')
& $cli energy export $project $a $b (Join-Path $delivery 'regenerated-evidence.zip')
```

To create and calculate another original example, use a fresh destination:

```powershell
$project = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA 'GreenLens/projects/new-energy-example'
& $cli energy example $project
& $cli energy run $project --alternative A
& $cli energy run $project --alternative B
```
