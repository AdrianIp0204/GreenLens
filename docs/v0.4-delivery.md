## Inspect and reproduce the delivered example

```powershell
$delivery = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA 'GreenLens/releases/0.4.0/20261013-energy-candidate-9b65897'
$cli = Join-Path $delivery 'GreenLens-CLI-0.4.0-windows-x64/greenlens.exe'
$project = Join-Path $delivery 'example-project'
$a = 'd7032960-4a14-4158-a276-af515bdf534a'
$b = '910f87da-3ba4-4edc-808a-0af2d5354a3e'
& $cli energy inspect $project $a
& $cli energy compare $project $a $b
& $cli energy rows $project $b --start 0 --count 144
& $cli energy package-inspect (Join-Path $delivery 'energy-example-evidence.zip')

# New report/export destinations are required; EnergyPlus is not needed:
& $cli energy report $project $a $b (Join-Path $delivery 'regenerated-reports')
& $cli energy export $project $a $b (Join-Path $delivery 'regenerated-evidence.zip')

# Replay performs new engine execution and requires the pinned EnergyPlus:
& $cli energy replay (Join-Path $delivery 'energy-example-evidence.zip')
```

