# v0.4 energy contracts (implementation in progress)

These contracts are independent of legacy daylight model/run identities. See the [specification](v0.4-energy-spec.md) and [implementation plan](v0.4-energy-implementation-plan.md). Current source is under development; declarations below are not acceptance evidence.

`greenlens-core::energy` owns a versioned thermal model, explicit calendar/schedules, energy weather admission, physical identities, accounting and the one `EnergyReportData` dataset. The runner's optional energy project extension stores A/B thermal designs, weather references and immutable energy-run references alongside existing daylight evidence. Importing weather for energy independently validates its thermal fields; a daylight asset does not confer energy suitability.

Energy series use `greenlens.energy-series.v0.4`: float64 little-endian, time-major then declared channel order. Each channel has a stable ID, descriptive label and explicit unit. At most 64 channels are retained in seven-day chunks. Production studies contain 52,560 ten-minute intervals; the separately declared five-minute sensitivity studies contain 105,120. Time is a complete non-leap local-standard year, interval-end, without sizing/warm-up rows. Chunk shape, order, safe path, SHA-256, finite values and unit ranges are checked on read. Result queries are capped at 2,016 intervals; no whole matrix is sent to the desktop.

At the maximum 64-channel ten-minute shape, numeric storage is 26,910,720 bytes per case before compression. An A/B numeric pair is 53,821,440 bytes; weather, model, metadata and reports require additional preflight. This calculation is a storage bound, not a measured resource or package claim. Every export still obeys the shared 5,000-entry, 64 MiB-per-entry and 256 MiB-total uncompressed limits.

`greenlens-process` owns native Windows Job creation for EnergyPlus and Typst. The Job is assigned before execution; cancellation, deadlines and resource failures terminate descendants. Stdout/stderr are byte files, not shell pipelines. A successful receipt requires exit zero and an empty process tree. Trusted compiled adapters supply argument arrays and explicitly chosen child environment overrides; imported recipes are data, never code. Inherited environment blocks and machine configuration are not serialized. Historical Radiance supervision and identities remain unchanged.

The run binds authoritative inputs, weather, complete engine/method/numerical identity, meter ledger, complete series, processed totals, process receipts, UTC times and source identity. It retains generated epJSON/recipe text and bounded raw-output representation. Large disposable native products are regenerated through the recognized adapter on replay. Promotion, writer locking, revision checks, pending-evidence recovery and Save As reuse existing project services. Inspection and report regeneration require compact evidence and the separate report renderer, but no EnergyPlus installation or original import path.

Execution completion, integrity-check scope, current-input relationship and exact-method scientific qualification are separate. Energy qualification defaults to unassessed until source-bound checks establish a limited scope. Neither successful execution nor a daylight assessment supplies an energy qualification.
