## Full reference parity after v0.4 (not the current completion target)

1. **Reference-scale thermal model:** benchmark at least 128 zones and multiple spaces per zone; allow more storeys, richer paired geometry, template/override management and complete inputs. Freeze memory/runtime/archive budgets with real tests. Bounded IFC ingestion needs its own unit/topology/semantic-loss/license checks; it is not necessary to prove the first energy release.
2. **Systems and complete accounting:** explicit VRF and other justified system families, heating/humidity/plant/pumps, electric DHW and lifts/escalators through validated models or documented exceptional calculations. Meter audit, services/unmet loads and performance curves precede savings claims. Annual-only supplementary calculations cannot be included in coincident demand. Add report annex generation for equipment evidence and complete applicability-aware totals.
3. **Hong Kong assessment module:** inventory current NB scheme/edition/circulars and project applicability, obtain calculation/evidence rights, implement separately versioned baseline/rules/rotations and OTTV/RTTV where required, and review with a competent domain professional. The module consumes supported simulations and identifies missing evidence. It may evaluate supported criteria; formal certification remains external.

Stages 1 and 2 require independent acceptance for larger mixed-use buildings and additional systems. Stage 3 requires its own regional applicability and evidence review. A professional-looking v0.4 report alone does not establish either. EnergyPlus capability in principle is not evidence GreenLens supports a particular system or code path.
