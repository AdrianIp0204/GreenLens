## Physical and metering contract

The native feasibility topology is a **recirculating** `ZoneHVAC:WindowAirConditioner`: `OutdoorAir:Mixer` with zero outdoor-air flow, a blow-through `Fan:SystemModel`, and `Coil:Cooling:DX:SingleSpeed` with an air-cooled condenser. Capacity, gross coil COP, gross SHR, airflow, all curve coefficients/domains, fan power/pressure/efficiency and sizing provenance are explicit. Gross coil COP includes compressor/condenser-fan electricity and excludes the indoor supply fan. Crankcase and basin heaters are disabled explicitly. Constant synthetic performance curves used in the feasibility fixture are not manufacturer data.

Independent outdoor air uses `ZoneVentilation:DesignFlowRate`, `Balanced`: equal intake/exhaust at the same schedule, equal fan pressure/efficiency, no heat recovery or detailed pressure-network claim. EnergyPlus adds intake fan heat to entering air and accounts for both fans' electricity. The AC does not deliver this outdoor air. Ventilation may continue while AC availability is zero. Unequal intake/exhaust is unsupported by this initial topology. Infiltration remains a distinct declared input.

Disjoint native J meters are `Cooling:Electricity`, `Fans:Electricity`, `InteriorLights:Electricity`, `ExteriorLights:Electricity` and `InteriorEquipment:Electricity`, reconciled to `Electricity:Facility`. Keyed `Fan Electricity Energy` and `Zone Ventilation Fan Electricity Energy` split supply versus balanced ventilation fans, while their sum must reconcile to `Fans:Electricity`. The window-AC aggregate already includes its supply fan: it is a crosscheck only, never another ledger contribution. `Cooling Coil Total Cooling Energy` is delivered thermal cooling, not electricity. Zone mean temperature and cooling-setpoint temperature are retained at zone timestep; occupancy comes from the exact expanded input schedule. Missing outputs are not zeros. Native names and output precision remain subject to the recorded feasibility run.

The application adapter streams interval-major values to the shared runner, with explicit channel identity/unit and Jan-1 local-standard interval-end indexing. Ten-minute and five-minute series have 52,560 and 105,120 rows. Native end-use reconciliation and shared storage bounds are unchanged from the specification. An HVAC availability fraction is an availability switch: zero is off and a positive value makes the system available; it does not scale capacity or continuous supply-fan power. Fractional people, lighting, equipment and ventilation schedules retain their respective load/flow semantics.

Scheduled cooling temperature and operative cutout temperature are distinct native outputs. `Schedule Value` must match the authoritative cooling schedule within 1e-7 C; this verified scheduled value defines comfort. `Zone Thermostat Cooling Setpoint Temperature` retains the actual operative value, including system-substep averaging within the declared schedule-minus-deadband to schedule range. The first production A run completed natively but failed the initial decoder at interval99/Z002 because that decoder compared operative23.8 C to scheduled24 C. The failed run is retained; the correction adds explicit schedule output and preserves the configured0.2 K cutout, all model physics and the comfort threshold. The fixed recipe hash changes with this output-contract correction. EnergyPlus's [cutout documentation](https://energyplus.readthedocs.io/en/v26.2.0/guides/input-output-reference/1.36-group---zone-controls---thermostats-and-humidistats.html) also warns that its own unmet-hours output has different semantics with this option; GreenLens computes its specified schedule-based measure independently.

## Weather and declared first fixtures

The existing Hong Kong PVGIS daylight EPW is **not energy-qualified**: its negative infrared/wind data are not silently repaired. Required energy fields must reject missing sentinels/nonfinite values and invalid physical ranges before the engine; EnergyPlus's fallback weather substitutions are not GreenLens acceptance. The first fixtures use an original Apache-2.0 synthetic 8,760-hour EPW, explicitly fictional: 35 C dry bulb, 20 C dew point, 41% RH, 101325 Pa, 400 Wh/m2 infrared, zero solar, 2 m/s wind and opaque/total sky cover 10. Its purpose is controlled diagnosis/resource measurement, not climatic representativeness. Rain and snow indicators are disabled explicitly. Real-weather rights and suitability remain a separate Gate 0 obligation.

Before native results, [the fixture generator](../scripts/energy-gate0-fixture.py) fixes four cases, each with immutable input/oracle hashes and one native process. This development-only Python generator is not an application runtime requirement. Timestep humidity ratio is derived from dry bulb, relative humidity and pressure; wet bulb then follows from that ratio. Supplied dew point is not the controlling latent-load humidity quantity, and the engine bounds its reported dew point by wet bulb. GreenLens's current range and `dew<=dry` admission is not a psychrometric-consistency certificate. All original fields remain unchanged; missing-field fallback remains disallowed. This explicit native derivation behavior must not be described as validation that arbitrary internally contradictory meteorological fields are mutually consistent.
