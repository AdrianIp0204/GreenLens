# GreenLens v0.4 energy analysis and reporting specification

Status: **implementation activated 2026-10-13; not yet verified or released**. Established 2026-10-12. This specification defines an independent energy workstream; it neither accepts v0.3 daylight nor reopens its exhausted scientific investigation. See [implementation plan](v0.4-energy-implementation-plan.md) and [roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Product outcome and reference boundary

An architect can define a small thermal building, create baseline and proposed designs, run real annual energy simulations, investigate energy and demand differences, and export an original GreenLens report with its complete input evidence and reproducibility package. International design comparison works without an assessment framework. Reports are engineering analysis, not certification.

Deliver an energy assessment, a simulation-input evidence appendix and a standalone end-use energy/demand comparison table. Use original synthetic examples and cleared evidence attachments. No private third-party copies, screenshots, branding, identifiers or project-specific values belong in public source or fixtures.

All prose, tables and charts must derive from one typed report dataset, with units and metric identity bound to each value. Acceptance uses authoritative complete model, weather, engine, system and output evidence; resemblance to any reference document's numerical results is not an acceptance criterion.

### Required deliverable families

| Deliverable | Required information | v0.4 boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building energy assessment report, PDF and standalone HTML | Cover/revision, contents, project/site, scope/method, baseline/proposed changes, model views, envelope/HVAC/gain/schedule tables, annual/monthly/end-use energy, operational emissions, coincident demand, charts, diagnostics, conclusions, evidence index, provenance and limitations | Complete for supported small all-electric models; no assessment points |
| Input simulation evidence appendix, PDF | Paired model views; zone/surface/assembly/glazing assignments; orientation WWR; gains, schedules, system/fan/sizing inputs; sources, assumptions, overrides and object IDs | Actual GreenLens model snapshots, not imitation of another application's dialogs; complete machine-readable inventory accompanies selected representative views |
| Performance comparison table, PDF/PNG/CSV and JSON | End use, fuel, process classification where explicitly supplied, baseline/proposed kWh, savings, demand contributions and savings, totals, interval and peak timestamps | Label as design comparison; never label an unassessed pair PRM compliance |
| Portable analysis package | Original weather, authoritative inputs, generated epJSON, retained native result evidence, normalized series, logs/diagnostics, identities, report dataset, reports, source/license records and checksums | Offline inspect/regenerate without engine or original import path; replay requires separately installed compatible EnergyPlus |

Manufacturer pages and design documents are user-supplied evidence, not generated simulation facts. v0.4 supports indexed local PDF/PNG/JPEG attachments with captions, scope and hashes; PDFs can accompany the package and be referenced in the report without mandatory page merging. Missing evidence remains visible. Export inclusion is explicit and excluded by default for private attachments. The public example uses only original synthetic evidence.

## Bounded first energy release

- One building, 1–8 rectangular prismatic thermal zones, at most two storeys, one space per zone as an explicit initial simplification. Support rectangular openings, fixed overhangs and detached box shading. Existing Rhino-inspired cameras, selection, command/Properties editing and display layers remain; no general CAD kernel or BIM authoring rewrite.
- Rust validates positive volumes, planarity/winding, non-overlap, matching full-face interzone boundaries, paired normals/areas and constructions, openings contained in their parent surface, boundary conditions, and assignments. Reject partial-face adjacency, unsupported topology and ambiguous boundary matches actionably. Exposed floors, roofs, exterior walls, paired interzone surfaces and explicitly adiabatic faces have distinct semantics. The initial ground boundary is an explicit insulated/adiabatic approximation; no ground-coupled physics claim.
- Baseline and proposed designs are manually defined, with stable zone/surface IDs and a computed differences list. In v0.4 retain equal zoning, gross floor area, site, weather, time/calendar, service levels and meter boundaries. Allow declared envelope, glazing/opening/shading, lighting and equipment/system parameter changes. Schedule/setpoint/occupancy changes that reduce service invalidate a like-for-like savings claim; show the difference rather than silently accepting it. Lighting-service equivalence is separately declared and supported by design/equipment evidence where available; lower lighting power does not prove equivalent illumination. Unresolved equivalence remains visible in savings conclusions, and failed/unqualified daylight methods cannot establish it.
- One complete non-leap hourly EPW year, explicit synthetic calendar/start weekday and holidays, fixed local standard time, no implicit OS timezone or DST. Run six zone timesteps per hour. Retain 52,560 aligned 10-minute energy/demand intervals and required comfort diagnostics, excluding sizing and warm-up periods. Hourly/monthly views are derived.
- Layered opaque constructions: thickness, conductivity, density, specific heat, absorptance/emissivity, source and assignment. Show film conventions for derived U-values. Simplified glazing: explicitly defined U-value, SHGC and visible transmittance with whole-window/glass/frame convention. Never substitute daylight reflectance/VLT for thermal properties or silently convert shading coefficient to SHGC.
- Distinct typed occupancy/activity, lighting, equipment, HVAC availability, outdoor-air/exhaust, infiltration and thermostat schedules. Support weekday/Saturday/Sunday profiles plus explicit holiday overrides and annual expansion. Include people density and sensible/latent/radiant conventions; equipment electrical input and heat fractions; ventilation and infiltration flow basis/units. Defaults are documented synthetic assumptions requiring review.
- One real, explicit air-cooled single-speed DX cooling system with a supply fan per conditioned zone. Include rated conditions, capacity/sizing, performance curves, thermostat/deadband, availability, part-load behavior, outdoor air and a documented exhaust/mass-balance route. Freeze exact EnergyPlus object topology and metering after the native gate. Heating, humidity control and annual heating energy are outside v0.4; label cooling-only scope and disclose hours outside supported service assumptions. No claim that this template models VRF.
- Use explicit sized capacities or a reviewed design-day sizing route; retain design conditions, sizing factors and resulting capacities. Do not infer design days or capacities from a report screenshot. One-zone IdealLoads is allowed for diagnostics, never as the delivered explicit-system qualification.
- Supported electrical end uses: DX cooling-system electricity, supply/AC fans, separate ventilation/exhaust fans where modeled, interior lighting, exterior lighting and plug/equipment. State whether cooling-system meters include condenser fans, crankcase heaters or other auxiliaries; call a value compressor-only only when its output actually isolates that component. Fans already included in a packaged system must not be counted again. Unexplained facility residuals remain visible and prevent complete end-use reconciliation. Heating, pumps, separate heat-rejection plants, hot water, lifts/escalators, gas, PV/storage and detailed process systems remain unsupported unless a later specification adds their physics/accounting.
- A report cell has a typed state: measured/simulated value (including true zero), not applicable, missing or unsupported. An unsupported end use present in the intended building prevents a complete-building claim. Default rows with zero do not establish that an end use was modeled.

The required demonstration is an original four-zone, two-storey model, two distinct use schedules, at least one interzone wall and floor/ceiling adjacency, fixed external shading, and meaningful baseline/proposed envelope, lighting and cooling/fan differences. It represents the complete declared synthetic building.

## Architecture and dependency decisions

Retain Rust/MSVC, Tauri 2, React/TypeScript/Three.js WebGL2, shared CLI/desktop Rust application services, one supervised active simulation job and local portable project storage. No mandatory Python, OpenStudio, Honeybee, application database, cloud or paid service.

1. Add a separate versioned thermal model/energy study contract in the Rust core. Preserve v0.1–v0.3 serialization, hash and replay dispatch. A daylight-to-thermal conversion may prefill geometry only and must request missing thermal inputs; it cannot manufacture a simulation-ready model.
2. Add a `greenlens-energyplus` adapter generating native epJSON and requesting bounded native CSV/JSON outputs. Plan to pin EnergyPlus 26.2.0, subject to actual Windows package/schema/license verification in Gate 0. Run through argument arrays and the existing process-tree supervisor. Do not accept arbitrary IDF/epJSON, imported executable commands, plugins, EMS/Python scripts or package-supplied recipes in this release.
3. Keep weather/material/carbon providers separate from the adapter. Add an **energy-specific EPW validation profile**: current daylight acceptance deliberately permits unused infrared/wind anomalies and is not thermal acceptance. Validate dry bulb, humidity/dew point, pressure, wind, radiation, sky/infrared inputs and every field used by the pinned engine. If a documented engine derivation is supported, record its exact prerequisites and normalization; otherwise reject missing/invalid required data. Preserve original bytes, normalized inputs, quality/source flags and license.
4. The runner owns jobs, immutable results, bounded time series, metering, comparison and a versioned `EnergyReportData` contract. Rust derives values and narrative facts. Frontend, HTML, PDF, CSV and JSON consume the same dataset. No frontend energy calculations or separate hand-maintained report totals.
5. Use a pinned native **Typst CLI** for canonical offline PDF generation, with original owned templates, local SVG/PNG assets and reviewed pinned fonts. Package/provision the renderer with checksums and notices; no runtime package download. Rust writes escaped structured JSON, invokes only the owned template, and constrains access to the report staging root. User strings and imported packages cannot become Typst source. HTML remains independently readable; a missing renderer blocks PDF completion, not result inspection. Gate 0 validates Windows/headless operation and asset/font/license scope before adoption. WebView2 printing may be a later convenience, not the sole headless export path.
6. Regional assessment modules remain separate evidence consumers. No BEAM/BEC thresholds, baseline generation, rotation averaging or credit claims enter the core. Future generic run-set contracts may support rotations; a validated assessment module must select the applicable rule and aggregation.

Physical simulation identity binds thermal geometry, constructions, gains/schedules, HVAC/curves/sizing, weather/normalization/calendar, engine and adapter/numerics. Occupancy and lighting schedules change energy physics, unlike a postprocessed daylight occupancy metric. Meter mapping/aggregation has a separate processing identity; carbon factors, area-normalization choice and report presentation have derived identities. Editing a carbon factor regenerates emissions without re-running thermal physics. Camera/panel state never affects science.

## Result and accounting contract

- Annual/monthly energy by end use and fuel, in kWh, with gross/conditioned area and EUI denominator named. Convert native joules using exactly 3,600,000 J/kWh. Do not confuse thermal cooling delivered with compressor electricity.
- `savings_percent = 100 * (baseline - proposed) / baseline`; a zero baseline produces undefined/N/A percentage, never infinity or invented 100%. Retain signed changes and negative savings.
- Operational emissions derive from energy and versioned fuel/region/year factors with units and source. Label kgCO2 versus kgCO2e precisely. No unsourced default factor. Missing factors suppress the affected total/savings, not the energy result. The acceptance example supplies a clearly synthetic factor plus one rights-reviewed real factor if available; no claim of local accounting validity without reviewed scope.
- Facility demand uses interval energy divided by interval duration. At each timestamp sum a disjoint, audited end-use mapping and reconcile it to the native facility electricity meter. `peak = max_t facility_demand[t]`; report each end use's contribution **at that case's facility peak** and its timestamp. Baseline and proposed peaks may occur at different times. Independent end-use maxima, if offered, are separate columns that are never summed into facility peak. Label this a 10-minute interval demand, not an instantaneous or utility billing demand.
- Process/non-process classification must be explicit and source-bound; unclassified is permitted and visible. Do not infer regional classification from a row name. Display totals only for complete disjoint scopes.
- Report per-zone and building occupied cooling-unmet hours, definition/tolerance, schedules and diagnostics; building hours are the union of relevant intervals, not the sum across zones. Retain 10-minute temperatures/setpoints for this check; hourly averaging must not hide excursions. Distinguish calculation success from adequate conditioning. No HVAC-efficiency saving claim based on unmet-load changes without a visible service warning.
- WWR is derived by facade orientation from gross wall and opening areas with a declared true-north/bin convention and frame basis; roofs/skylights are separate. Calculations use full precision; presentation rounding is explicit. Rounding differences never change underlying totals or claims.
- Future exceptional calculations need formulas, units, assumptions, sources, ownership and overlap exclusions. Annual-only exceptional energy cannot support peak demand without an aligned time profile. v0.4 identifies such required inputs as unsupported; it does not fabricate them or merely add annual scalars to claim whole-building parity.

## Evidence, safety and usability

Every completed run records input/engine/adapter/schema hashes, relevant settings/arguments, exact engine version, full successful completion, required output identities/counts, diagnostics/logs and finite results. Fatal/severe errors, missing meters, partial years or malformed results prevent successful finalization. Capture and classify warnings; unresolved comfort/sizing/physical warnings are visible in report conclusions and qualification. Do not silently suppress or whitelist an unfamiliar warning.

Separate execution, integrity, current-input relationship and exact-method scientific qualification in CLI, desktop and every report. Energy qualification is independent of failed daylight qualification. Adapter agreement proves integration only; limited analytical checks are not a validation claim for arbitrary buildings. Changed inputs retain historical reports/runs and mark them stale appropriately.

Persist compact immutable evidence with relative paths; scratch/builds/active outputs remain under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/GreenLens`. Use the existing Save As, conflict recovery, pending-evidence and atomic promotion contracts. Retain archive limits of 5,000 entries, 64 MiB per entry and 256 MiB total uncompressed. Stream bounded outputs and chunks; preflight the supported maximum and report selection. A successful export must pass the shared reader. Do not silently raise legacy limits or discard mandatory evidence to fit. Attachment inclusion cannot evade limits, safe-path validation or license/privacy review.

CLI must validate/run/inspect/compare/export/replay the same studies as desktop. Desktop must create/edit the thermal example, manage both designs and thermal assignments, import weather, configure schedules/systems, run/cancel, inspect result/source/diagnostic tables and charts, and export all deliverables. Use the existing Rhino-inspired workspace and Rust-validated transactions, not a disconnected reporting app. Tabular thermal inputs complement the viewport. Accepted edits obey undo/redo, lock and save protection; native text shortcuts remain intact.

PDFs must have readable tables, repeated headings, units, figure/table captions, page numbers and correctly referenced sections. Dense input tables may use landscape pages and representative PDF views, but complete inputs remain in JSON/CSV. Both reports and the standalone performance table must carry model/run IDs, scope, evidence status and limitations. Headless export uses deterministic original model/evidence views, such as Rust-generated SVG projections; desktop screenshots may supplement them but cannot be required for CLI output or substitute for input tables.

## Acceptance (targets, not reported results)

1. Native Windows EnergyPlus and offline PDF feasibility pass from paths containing spaces/non-ASCII. Exact versions/assets/licenses are recorded; no simulator or reporting engine is falsely claimed installed from file presence alone.
2. The four-zone demonstration runs both cases for a full year with explicit DX/fan systems, all required 52,560 intervals, correct environment separation and native diagnostics. All supported end uses and assumptions appear in reports. A one-zone diagnostic or mocked result cannot satisfy this item.
3. Hand-calculated fixtures cover units, schedules/calendars, layer resistance, WWR, fuel factors, missing data, zero denominators, negative savings, process scope, coincident/noncoincident peaks, unequal peak timestamps, rounding and comfort union. Small normalized arithmetic agrees within 1e-9; large-series aggregation agrees within `max(1e-9 absolute, 1e-12 relative)` against an independent compensated calculation.
4. Direct native execution of identical generated inputs and recognized replay agree with adapter annual/monthly/end-use energy and interval demand within `max(1e-6 in the reported unit, 0.1% reference)`, with exact labels, units, counts and time indices. Native end-use/facility energy reconciliation uses `max(0.01 kWh, 0.01%)`, and demand reconciliation `max(0.001 kW, 0.01%)`. Document output precision explicitly; change output precision or diagnose mapping before considering any contract change.
5. Before tuning, freeze independent fixtures: an exact scheduled electrical-load case and a steady-state envelope case with controlled boundary/film conditions. The latter must agree with its independently derived heat-flow target within `max(1 W, 1%)`; document all excluded effects and steady-state criterion. Verify interzone surface pairing/conservation. Preserve failures and do not present same-engine agreement as independent physics evidence.
6. Freeze a 10-minute versus 5-minute timestep study for both demonstration designs. Aggregate both to the same 10-minute demand intervals; annual facility electricity difference must be <=1%, facility peak difference <=3%. Neither threshold may be relaxed after results are seen without a separately reviewed specification revision. Investigate failures finitely and report any unresolved method qualification.
7. The declared demonstration has <=1% occupied cooling-unmet hours per conditioned zone at a 0.2 C cooling tolerance, with hours and denominator reported. This is an internal synthetic-fixture gate, not a BEAM/ASHRAE rule or a universal rejection of users' underconditioned studies. Native unmet-hours quantities are crosschecked only where their definitions match.
8. Both PDF families, standalone performance PDF/PNG/CSV/JSON and HTML derive from the same full-precision report contract. Automated checks verify bindings and totals; visually render and inspect every example PDF page, including long tables and missing-data states. No placeholder charts, copied results or invented professional sign-off.
9. Installed Windows app and headless CLI produce the deliverables offline; real cancellation kills the process tree. Save/copy/reopen/regenerate works without original import paths/cache/engine; replay works after separately installing the pinned engine. Test stale inputs, undo/currentness, save conflicts, corrupt/path-unsafe archives, unknown schemas and oversized exports.
10. Exact-source Windows CI, license/package inventory, installer, launch instructions and compact evidence are delivered. Energy acceptance is tracked separately from historical daylight failures; retain legacy regressions and do not delete/xfail failed qualification checks to obtain a green badge. Evidence explicitly distinguishes implemented, verified, unverified and blocked work.
