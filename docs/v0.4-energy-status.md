## Reproducible source commands

Clean candidate `2e11b1d` additionally passed [all-page revision-2 report review](evidence/v0.4-energy/revision2-clean-report-audit.json), [cold export and distributed replay](evidence/v0.4-energy/revision2-clean-delivery-audit.json), [build/notices checks](evidence/v0.4-energy/candidate-2e11-build.json) and [CLI ZIP/generated installer mapping inspection](evidence/v0.4-energy/candidate-2e11-package-audit.json). This preserves a usable inspectable checkpoint, not an installed release or coverage of the later fan example.

From PowerShell in the checkout:

```powershell
. ./scripts/dev-env.ps1
./scripts/install-energyplus.ps1
./scripts/install-typst.ps1
cargo build -p greenlens-cli
$cli = Join-Path $env:CARGO_TARGET_DIR 'debug/greenlens.exe'
$project = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA 'GreenLens/projects/energy-example'
& $cli energy doctor
& $cli energy example $project
& $cli energy run $project --alternative A
& $cli energy run $project --alternative B
```

The example destination must be empty. Read actual run IDs from `project.json` under `energy.runs`; pass A and B explicitly to `energy compare`, `energy report`, `energy export` and `energy replay`. Reports can be regenerated without EnergyPlus; PDF/PNG require the pinned separately installed Typst/fonts. Inspection and HTML-only output require neither engine. Normal calculation is offline. Original weather bytes travel with the project.

```powershell
$document = Get-Content (Join-Path $project 'project.json') -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json
$a = ($document.energy.runs | Where-Object alternative_id -eq A | Select-Object -Last 1).id
$b = ($document.energy.runs | Where-Object alternative_id -eq B | Select-Object -Last 1).id
& $cli energy inspect $project $a
& $cli energy rows $project $a --start 0 --count 144
& $cli energy report $project $a $b "$project-reports"
& $cli energy export $project $a $b "$project-evidence.zip"
& $cli energy package-inspect "$project-evidence.zip"
& $cli energy replay "$project-evidence.zip"
```

These are implementation commands, not claims that every final-release workflow has passed. Output destinations must be new. [The progress record](progress.md) and [native method](v0.4-energy-method.md) identify performed checks. The unsigned 0.4 installed package and exact-source CI are not yet accepted.
