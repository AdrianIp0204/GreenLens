## Finite resources and stopping rules

One missing production simulation (B2), then two explicitly declared replay simulations (A1/B2). No additional simulation for cache or report testing. Per engine invocation: 300-second collector deadline, 2 GiB runner/job working-set guard and 2 GiB scratch guard, in addition to stricter existing adapter limits. Per issue render/regeneration: 600-second collector deadline, existing supervised Typst limits, maximum 252 products, 64 MiB per member. Complete export: 5,000 entries and 256 MiB uncompressed. Record actual wall time, memory, scratch and output sizes; do not infer them from limits.

A software/collector failure may be repaired without silently accepting partial output. Preserve the failed attempt and do not rerun a successfully completed annual calculation merely to refresh report code or documentation. A scientific failure is retained without retuning. An oversized package fails before publication; do not silently prune earlier issues or assets. The separate improved native cancellation protocol has not yet been activated by this declaration.
