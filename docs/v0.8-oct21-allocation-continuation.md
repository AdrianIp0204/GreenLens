accounting does not intercept all TLV/model/scene copies, standard-container

uses default callbacks for its ordinary allocations. The final MSVC/UCRT,

runtime imports. This is concrete evidence of unrouted allocation paths, not the
final linked dependency closure or a measured maximum request. No worker was
linked or started to obtain this inventory.

remaining owners, with explicit failure semantics (including checked calloc and
realloc preserving the old allocation on failure), followed by auditing the exact
linked runtime. A bounded arena or a lower Job limit alone would not prove that
unrouted native commitment fits in the remaining headroom. No arbitrary arena or
guard is selected here. Only a justified complete bound can support declaring
the existing finite CT1 cases. All production ceilings and the retained failed
