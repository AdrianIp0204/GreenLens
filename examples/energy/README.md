# Original synthetic thermal example

The current factory is **example revision 3**, a candidate input revision. Relative to revision 2, baseline A is unchanged and only `zones[*].cooling_system.supply_fan.total_efficiency` changes from 0.5 to 0.6 in the four proposed B zones. Rated airflow, pressure, availability, motor efficiency and motor heat fraction remain unchanged. The explicit design electrical power is therefore 60 W per baseline fan and 50 W per proposed fan. This closes the demonstration's previously missing fan-input difference; it does not predict annual savings, because changed fan heat can also change cooling demand.

The frozen revision 2 generator remains available as `synthetic_four_zone_model_revision_two`. It changes only `zones[*].cooling_system.cooling_deadband_k` from 0.2 K to 0 K in every A/B zone relative to [revision 1 inputs](revision1-inputs.json). The original regression restores those eight cutout values and compares every other typed model leaf, calendar and weather binding. The revision 3 regression requires complete A equality, restores only the four B fan efficiencies to require complete revision 2 equality, checks 60/50 W design power, and checks that B's model identity changes. No duplicate historical input dataset is necessary.

Revision 1's annual B peak timestep failure remains authoritative. [Revision 2 full-year results](../../docs/evidence/v0.4-energy/revision2-production-timestep.json) support only their four exact physical/method scopes; they do not qualify revision 3 B or demonstrate a fan difference. Revision 3 is bounded to one declared A10, B10, A5 and B5 annual attempt, with 52,560 or 105,120 intervals respectively, the same weather/calendar/method, aligned ten-minute demand, unchanged 1% annual-energy and 3% peak timestep bands, and unchanged occupied comfort criteria. The separate protocol must be sealed before native execution, with the original per-process and scratch limits. No retry, additional variant, threshold change or qualification follows from this README.

The authoritative generator is `greenlens_core::energy::synthetic_four_zone_model`, with the calendar from `synthetic_energy_calendar` and weather bytes from `weather::synthetic_energy_epw`. It creates four rectangular zones in two storeys: two 8 × 6 × 3 m zones per storey, 192 m² gross and conditioned floor area, and reciprocal full-face internal walls and floors. Coordinates are building-local with an explicit true-north rotation. Every exposed south wall has one 3 × 1.5 m opening. The original synthetic location is latitude 0°, longitude 0°, elevation 0 m, UTC+00:00; the weather is an artificial repeated day, not measurements or a typical climatic year.

The baseline and proposed cases share geometry, calendar, occupancy, equipment, conditioning schedules, setpoints and balanced ventilation. The proposed inputs change insulation thickness, simplified glazing U-value and SHGC, fixed overhangs, lighting power, gross rated coil COP and supply-fan total efficiency. These are illustrative parameter differences, not a claimed energy improvement. Lower lighting power does not establish equal illumination. Equipment performance curves are explicitly identified original synthetic coefficients, not calibrated manufacturer data. Sizing is an explicit illustrative rated capacity; no hidden autosizing or IdealLoads substitution is permitted.

The non-leap calendar starts Monday and explicitly treats January 1 as a holiday. Office and evening zones have different weekday schedules, explicit weekend fractions and explicit holiday values. All source schedules contain 144 ten-minute values per day. The sensitivity route repeats those values at five-minute intervals rather than interpolating them.

`synthetic_energy_report()` is a separate arithmetic/rendering fixture. Its full-year constant electrical series produce 8,760 kWh and 7,008 kWh by construction; those numbers are not simulation results. Its four assessment axes explicitly record **not executed**, **not integrity checked**, **not compared** and **unassessed**. Its absent carbon factor remains missing, not zero. Do not count this fixture as engine, model, native desktop or scientific acceptance.

All inputs and generator code are original Apache-2.0 project material. No weather database, manufacturer model, logo or external simulation output is embedded here. Authoritative runtime semantics are in Rust; the [thermal schema](../../schemas/thermal-model-v0.4.schema.json), [study schema](../../schemas/energy-study-v0.4.schema.json), [weather schema](../../schemas/energy-weather-v0.4.schema.json), [report schema](../../schemas/energy-report-v0.4.schema.json) and [presentation schema](../../schemas/energy-report-presentation-v0.4.schema.json) document their portable JSON shapes.
